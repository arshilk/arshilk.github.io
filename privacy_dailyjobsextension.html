Privacy Policy

Decibel Studios built the DailyJobs.ai: Job Autofill & Tracker Chrome extension (the "Extension"). The Extension is provided by Decibel Studios free of charge, with an optional paid Pro upgrade, and is intended for use as is.

This page is used to inform users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information if anyone decides to use the Extension. This policy covers the Chrome extension only. The DailyJobs.ai website and the DailyJobs Android app have their own privacy policy at https://dailyjobs.ai/privacy.

If you choose to use the Extension, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. We will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy.

Information Collection and Use

The Extension helps you fill in job applications and keep track of the jobs you apply to. To do that it stores the information you give it, and it is designed so that all of this information stays in your browser. It is saved using Chrome's extension storage on your device and is not collected by us in any way. Decibel Studios does not operate any server that receives data from the Extension, and the Extension does not send your profile, resumes, answers or tracked jobs to DailyJobs.ai or anyone else.

Information the Extension stores locally, only if you enter or create it:

  • Your profile: name, preferred name, pronouns, email address, phone number, date of birth, postal address, links (LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio, website, X), current job title and company, years of experience, headline, summary, skills, languages, work authorization and visa sponsorship answers, relocation and workplace preferences, salary expectation, notice period, earliest start date, and how you heard about a company.
  • Optional voluntary self-identification (EEO) answers: gender, Hispanic or Latino origin, race or ethnicity, veteran status and disability status. These are optional, are used only to answer the same voluntary questions on application forms, and can be left blank or set to "Prefer not to say".
  • Work experience and education entries.
  • Resumes: the resume files you upload (PDF, DOC, DOCX or TXT) and the plain text of your resume.
  • Answer bank and cover letter: questions and answers you save, answers the Extension remembers for you (see "Remembered Answers" below), and your cover letter template.
  • Job tracker: jobs you save or apply to (job title, company, location, salary, job link, job description), their status, dates, and any notes you add.
  • Preferences: theme, language and autofill settings, the names of websites you switched autofill or saving off for, and your Pro license status.
  • Free plan usage: a count of how many autofills you have used (the free plan includes 50). It is only a number, stored on your device, and is never sent anywhere.

The only information that leaves your device is described in the "Pro Upgrade and Payments" section below (your license key, sent to our payment provider to verify a Pro purchase). The Extension contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no tracking.

Link to privacy policy of third-party service providers used by the Extension:

  • Dodo Payments (checkout and license verification for the optional Pro upgrade)
  • WhatsApp (only if you choose to contact us from the Extension's Help & Community section)

Browser Permissions

The Extension requests the following permissions to provide its core functionality:

  • Storage and unlimited storage (storage, unlimitedStorage): Used to save your profile, resume files, answers, tracked jobs and preferences locally on your device. Unlimited storage is needed because resume files can be larger than Chrome's default storage quota.
  • Access to supported job and application websites (host permissions and content scripts): Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, iCIMS, Workable, BambooHR, Breezy HR, Recruitee, Jobvite, Taleo, SuccessFactors, Teamtailor, Personio, Rippling, Dover, JazzHR, ADP Workforce Now, LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri and Foundit. On these sites the Extension shows its Autofill / Save button, fills application forms when you ask it to (or automatically, if you turn that on), reads the job title, company, location, salary and description so you can save the job, and notices when an application has been submitted so the job can be marked as Applied. On LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri and Foundit it only acts on job pages and apply forms, never on feeds, messages or profiles.
  • Active tab and scripting (activeTab, scripting): Used to read and fill the application form on the tab you are using when you click the Extension, use its keyboard shortcut or its right-click menu, including on websites that are not in the list above. The Extension only acts on a page when you ask it to there.
  • Context menus (contextMenus): Adds "Autofill this application" and "Save this job to DailyJobs" to the right-click menu.
  • Alarms (alarms): Used to periodically re-check the Pro license with our payment provider so that cancelled subscriptions and refunded purchases correctly return to the free version.
  • Access to live.dodopayments.com (host permission): Used only to activate, validate, and deactivate a Pro license key through Dodo Payments' license API.

How Autofill Works

When you autofill a form, the Extension looks at the form's field labels in your browser, matches them with your locally stored profile, answers and resume, and types the matching values into the form on your screen. Your resume file is attached only to a resume or CV upload field. The Extension never submits a form for you; you review it and press submit yourself. It does not fill passwords, and it never ticks consent or terms checkboxes on your behalf.

If you turn on "Autofill as soon as an application opens" (off by default), the same filling happens automatically when an application form opens on a supported job website. Please always check a form before you submit it.

Remembered Answers

If "Remember my answers" is on (it can be turned off in Settings), when you answer a new question on an application form yourself and then press Next, Continue or Submit, the Extension saves that question and your answer to your local answer bank so it can fill the same question next time. The company name and job title in a saved answer are replaced with placeholders so the answer fits the next company. The Extension never remembers passwords, government ID or social security numbers, passport details, bank or card details, or date of birth, and it never remembers fields that already come from your profile. Remembered answers are stored only on your device and can be edited or deleted in the Answer bank at any time.

Reading Your Resume

The "Fill from resume" feature reads the resume file you choose and fills empty fields of your profile (such as your name, contact details, work experience, education and skills). The file is read entirely inside your browser using the bundled, open-source pdf.js library (for PDF files) or the browser's built-in tools (for DOCX and TXT files). Your resume is not uploaded anywhere.

Per-Site Choices

From the Extension's popup you can turn autofill off for a website, and turn off saving for a website (no remembered answers and no Applied tracking there). These choices are stored on your device as a list of website names, which you can review and reset in Settings.

Job Tracker

When you save a job, or when the Extension notices that you submitted an application on a supported site, it stores the job's title, company, location, salary, link and description in your local job tracker. On the free plan, submitted applications only update jobs that are already in your tracker; with Pro they are also added. You can edit, delete, export (Pro) or import jobs at any time. Exported CSV files are created in your browser and downloaded to your device.

Pro Upgrade and Payments

The Extension offers an optional Pro upgrade (monthly subscription, yearly subscription, or lifetime purchase). Payments are processed by Dodo Payments on their secure checkout page. We do not collect, store, or have access to your card or payment details.

After purchase, Dodo Payments issues a license key, which is shown on the checkout return page and emailed to you. To unlock Pro, the Extension sends the following to Dodo Payments' license API:

  • Your license key and an activation name ("DailyJobs for Chrome"), when you activate Pro;
  • Your license key and its activation ID, when the Extension periodically re-checks that the license is still valid (for example when you open the Extension, when Chrome starts, and about once an hour in the background);
  • Your license key and its activation ID, when you choose "Remove from this browser", or when a key for a different product is entered.

When a license is activated, Dodo Payments returns the purchased plan, the email address used for the purchase, and the activation date. The Extension stores these locally on your device only to show your plan details. They are not sent anywhere else. Your profile, resumes, answers and tracked jobs are never sent to Dodo Payments. Dodo Payments' handling of your data is governed by its own privacy policy, linked above.

Backup and Restore

The Extension's Backup & Restore feature exports the data you choose (profile, resumes, answers, tracked jobs and settings) to a file that is downloaded to your device, and can import such a file. Backup files are created and read entirely within your browser; the Extension does not upload them anywhere. Because a backup file can contain your personal details and resumes, keep it somewhere safe. Backup files do not include your license key.

Contact, Sharing and External Links

If you use "Contact Us", the Extension opens WhatsApp (wa.me) in a new tab with the message you typed, so you can send it yourself; the Extension does not send or store that message. "Share" copies a short message with a link to the Extension to your clipboard. Links to DailyJobs.ai, YouTube, Instagram, Google Play, the Chrome Web Store, and our other apps simply open those websites in a new tab.

Log Data

The Extension does not collect log data, crash reports, or usage statistics. If something goes wrong, error details are written only to your browser's local developer console and are never transmitted.

Cookies

The Extension does not use cookies. Websites you visit, including job websites and the Dodo Payments checkout page, may use their own cookies according to their own policies.

Your data is used only to provide the Extension's single purpose of filling in job applications and tracking the jobs you apply to. We do not sell user data, we do not use or transfer it for purposes unrelated to that purpose, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use or transfer it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. No human at Decibel Studios can read your data, because it never leaves your device.

Data Retention and Deletion

All data the Extension stores lives in your browser until you delete it. You can edit or delete any profile field, resume, answer or tracked job in Settings and the Job Tracker, and "Delete all my data" in Settings removes your profile, resumes, answers and tracked jobs at once. Remove your license with "Remove from this browser". Uninstalling the Extension deletes all of its locally stored data. To ask Dodo Payments about purchase records, contact Dodo Payments or use the details in your purchase receipt email.
