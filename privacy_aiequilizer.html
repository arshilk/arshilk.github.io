Device Permissions

The app may request the following device permissions to provide its core functionality:

  • Modify Audio Settings: Required to apply the equalizer, bass boost, virtualizer, loudness, limiter, noise gate, compressor, and multiband-compression effects to the system audio output. No audio content is captured, recorded, or transmitted.
  • Foreground Service (Special Use - Audio): Used to keep the audio processing engine running in the background so the equalizer continues to apply effects even when the app is not in the foreground. A persistent notification is shown while the service is active.
  • Notifications: Used to display the foreground service status, the persistent enable/disable notification controls, and to deliver in-app status updates.
  • Vibrate: Used for tactile feedback when interacting with sliders, toggles, and other touch controls.
  • Notification Listener (Recap feature, optional): If you enable the Recap feature, the app reads MediaSession metadata published by other apps (now-playing title, artist, album art, source application) to build your personal listening history, top songs, time-listened statistics, and liked tracks. This data is processed and stored entirely on your device. Notification content other than MediaSession metadata is not read, stored, or transmitted. The Recap feature is opt-in — Notification Access must be granted manually in the system settings, and revoking it immediately stops all collection. Uninstalling the app deletes the local Recap database.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): Used by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to serve personalized advertisements to non-premium users in supported regions. Users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, and applicable US states are presented with a consent form managed by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalized ads are served. Users can update their ad personalization choice at any time via the in-app "Privacy options" entry inside Help & FAQ.
  • Billing: Used to process in-app purchases (monthly subscription, annual subscription, and lifetime premium upgrade) through Google Play Billing.

Backup and Restore

The app provides a Backup & Restore feature that exports your saved equalizer profiles, settings, and preferences to a file of your choice on your device or a cloud storage location you select (such as Google Drive). The app does not upload your backup data to any server controlled by Decibel Studios. Backup files contain only your local app configuration; no personally identifiable information is included.

Headphone EQ Database

The app includes a bundled database of headphone calibration profiles derived from the open-source AutoEq project. This database is shipped with the app and queried entirely on-device; no headphone selection or usage data is transmitted.
