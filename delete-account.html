Delete Your Account

DailyJobs: Job Search App (ai.dailyjobs.app)

How to delete your account

  1. Open the DailyJobs: Job Search App on your Android device.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Confirm the deletion in the dialog that appears.

Alternatively, you can request account deletion by emailing me@arshilk.com from the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 7 days.

What data is deleted

Data type Action
Account profile (name, email) Permanently deleted
Job search preferences and presets Permanently deleted
Saved job statuses and history Permanently deleted
Authentication tokens Permanently deleted
All data is deleted immediately upon account deletion. There is no additional retention period. If you have an active subscription, please cancel it through Google Play before deleting your account.
