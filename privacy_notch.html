Device Permissions

The app may request the following device permissions to provide its core functionality:

  • Accessibility Service: Required to draw the gesture overlay around the camera cutout and to execute system actions you bind to gestures (Home, Back, Recents, Screenshot, Notification shade, Quick settings, Power menu, Split screen, Lock screen). The service is used only to render the overlay and perform actions you trigger. No screen content, typed text, or personal data is read, stored, or transmitted.
  • Notification Listener: Used to peek media playback information (now-playing title, artist, album art) and, optionally, notification previews around the notch. Notification content is rendered on-device only and is not stored or transmitted.
  • Notifications: Used to display service status, scheduled enable/disable alerts, and update prompts.
  • Camera: Used solely to control the device flashlight via the Camera Manager. No images or videos are captured or stored.
  • Microphone: Used only if you bind a gesture to the Record Audio action. Recordings are saved to your device and never uploaded.
  • Contacts & Phone: Used only if you bind a gesture to Speed Dial. Contact data stays on the device.
  • Modify System Settings: Used to change brightness and rotation when those actions are bound to a gesture.
  • Do Not Disturb Access: Used to toggle DND or ringer mode when those actions are bound to a gesture.
  • Bluetooth: Used to detect Bluetooth device connections so the notch can show a connect/disconnect peek.
  • Boot Completed & Exact Alarms: Used to restore the overlay after restart and to run scheduled enable/disable windows.
  • Biometric Authentication: Used optionally to secure app access. Biometric data is handled entirely by the device and is never accessed or stored by the app.
